Understanding a SYN flood incident

Google course exerciseTCP · Incident reporting

A website becomes unavailable while its server receives many TCP connection requests that don't complete.

Reported traffic

The report describes SYN packets from 203.0.113.0 to 192.0.2.1, followed by SYN-ACK replies but no final ACKs. This matches the course's SYN flood scenario.

The incomplete handshake

PacketNormal connectionReported traffic
SYNClient requests a connection.Many requests arrive.
SYN-ACKServer responds.The server sends replies.
ACKClient completes the handshake.The final acknowledgements are missing.

Incomplete connections can fill the server's connection backlog, depending on its implementation and protections. Other clients may then see delays or timeouts.

What to check

  • A capture with a known time window and capture location.
  • Counts of SYNs, completed handshakes and retransmissions compared with normal traffic.
  • Server metrics for backlog pressure and dropped connections.
  • Packet loss or capture visibility that could explain missing ACKs.

The report doesn't measure the attack rate or resource exhaustion. A source IP alone doesn't identify the sender or prove the traffic was distributed.

Possible response

I would review SYN flood protections, connection limits and upstream filtering with the service owner, then monitor the effect on legitimate traffic. These measures weren't deployed or tested in the exercise.
